# Until Payday

## Short description

How much you can spend each day until payday. Works offline, no sign-up.

## Full description

Until Payday answers one question: how much can you spend today and still make
it to your next paycheck?

No categories, no receipts, no bank connection. You enter your current balance
and your next payday, and the app works out a safe daily amount.

How it works:

1. Create a plan: enter your current balance and your next payday.
2. See your daily allowance and what your balance should be today if you are on
   track.
3. Check in your actual balance whenever you like. Your original plan is not
   reset, so you can see whether you are ahead or behind.
4. On payday, start a new period.

Why it works this way:

• Your numbers stay on your device. The app never sends them anywhere, every
  calculation runs locally, and it works with no connection at all.
• No accounts. Nothing to register, nothing to confirm.
• No analytics and no trackers of our own. One small banner keeps the app
  free, and it appears only after you have used the app on ten separate days.
• Data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall.
• Available in Ukrainian and English.
• Built for phones: large controls, zoom support, and screen reader support.

The app performs arithmetic on numbers you type in yourself. It is not a
banking service, it does not give investment advice, and it has no access to
your accounts.
